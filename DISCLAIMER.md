# Disclaimer & Responsible Use

This repository is for educational mobile application security research on intentionally vulnerable training challenges, such as those published by OWASP MAS.

- Test only apps and devices you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.
- Do not apply techniques from these exercises to third-party apps without permission.
- Do not publish personal information, real secrets, access tokens, or unrelated proprietary data.
- Do not redistribute challenge binaries unless the original license and distribution terms clearly allow it.
- Write-ups are provided as learning notes and may be incomplete or version-specific.

The OWASP name and challenge materials belong to their respective owners. This repository is independent and is not endorsed by OWASP.
